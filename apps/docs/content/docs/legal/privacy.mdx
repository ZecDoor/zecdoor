## What we never receive

- Your recovery words, private keys or viewing keys. They stay in your browser; a wallet made in ZecDoor keeps only its
  viewing key, in your browser's storage.
- Your transactions to sign. They are built in your browser and signed in Phantom.
- Your Solana wallet address for screening. The sanctions list is downloaded and checked in your browser.

## What our server handles, and keeps

| Request | What it contains | What we keep |
|---|---|---|
| Health and region checks | Your IP address and the country our host derives from it | Nothing |
| Sanctions list download | Your IP address | Nothing |
| Counting a finished move | The move's NEAR Intents deposit address, its kind, and whether it was a first wallet's first move. Our server then reads that move from NEAR Intents, which includes your Solana address, your Zcash address and the amounts. | Per-day totals only, plus a salted one-way hash of the deposit address for 24 hours to avoid double counting. No address, transaction ID, IP address or device ID is stored. |

**No cookies. No analytics. No tracking scripts.** The landing page stores your light/dark choice in your browser.

## What your browser shares directly with others

To make a move, your browser talks directly to these services. Each sees your IP address and what you ask it about:

- **NEAR Intents (1Click):** your Solana address (as refund address), your Zcash address, amounts. Its terms say it collects
  IP and wallet addresses. **Its public explorer shows the recipient address and amounts of public swaps.**
- **A Solana RPC provider** (rpc.solanatracker.io or public.rpc.solanavibestation.com): your Solana address and balances.
- **Jupiter** (top-up only): the swap you request and your Solana address. Jupiter logs IP and country.
- **A lightwalletd server** (zjs.zec.rocks): which Zcash block ranges your browser downloads. Not your keys.
- **Phantom:** under Phantom's own policy.

## Your device

This browser keeps, until you clear it: the viewing key and next address number of a wallet made here, a list of hashed
addresses you used before (to warn about reuse), and your move history. "Forget this wallet" removes the viewing key.

## Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you; since we keep no personal data beyond the above, there will usually be nothing to
return. EU and UK users may also complain to their data protection authority.
