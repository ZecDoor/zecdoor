---
title: What stays public
description: Exactly what a ZecDoor move reveals, and what it does not.
---

ZecDoor moves your ZEC into Zcash's shielded pool. The route there is partly public, and we would rather you know exactly
how.

## Public

- **Your Solana transfer.** The amount, the time, and your Solana wallet, like every Solana transaction.
- **The pool entry.** NEAR Intents pays from a transparent Zcash address into the shielded pool. The amount and time of that
  transaction are visible on Zcash.
- **The link between your Solana wallet and the Zcash address paid.** NEAR Intents' public explorer shows, for each swap,
  the deposit, the amounts and the recipient address ([sources](/sources#explorer)). Anyone who looks can see that your
  Solana wallet paid that Zcash address.
- **That the move went through ZecDoor.** Every ZecDoor move pays the same fee account on NEAR, so the explorer can group
  ZecDoor moves together.

## Private

- **Everything after arrival.** Once the ZEC is in the shielded pool, payments between shielded addresses do not reveal
  sender, recipient or amount on chain.
- **Your other addresses.** A wallet made in ZecDoor pays a new address of it on each move. The explorer links your Solana
  wallet to the addresses it paid, not to the rest of your wallet.

## Habits that help

- **Wait** before moving the ZEC again. Moving it straight back out links the two moves by time.
- **Don't send the exact amount out.** Round amounts blend in.
- **Pay shielded addresses** where you can.
- **Use a new address** for each move if you paste your own.

## What ZecDoor itself learns

See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). In short: our server stores no address, transaction ID, IP address or device ID.
The browser talks directly to NEAR Intents, a Solana RPC provider, Jupiter (top-up only) and a lightwalletd server; each of
those sees your IP address and what you ask it about.
