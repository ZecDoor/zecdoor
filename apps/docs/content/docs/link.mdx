ZecDoor offers a link and a badge, nothing that runs inside your page. Every link opens ZecDoor at its one address,
`zecdoor.0xo.in`, where the user connects their own wallet and chooses where their ZEC lands.

## The link

```text
https://zecdoor.0xo.in
```

To show a holder what an address holds and whether moving it is worth it, link to the [wallet check](/check). It needs no
wallet and only reads public data:

```text
https://zecdoor.0xo.in/app/#/check/<Solana address>
```

The badges are plain SVG files with no script. Loading one is a request to our host, as with any image; we turn on no
request logging of our own ([privacy](/legal/privacy)).

## What we do not offer, and why

- **No embed.** ZecDoor refuses to load inside another site's frame (`frame-ancestors 'none'` on every page). A wallet
  screen inside someone else's page is how phishing works: the user could not see whose address bar they are in.
- **No prefilled destination or amount.** No link can choose the Zcash address your ZEC goes to, or how much is moved.
  The user picks both inside ZecDoor and sees them on the review screen before signing.
- **No referral codes or tracking parameters.** ZecDoor reads none.

Linking to ZecDoor does not make you a partner of ZecDoor, and ZecDoor is not endorsed by NEAR Intents, Phantom or the
Zcash organisations it names.
