---
title: Buy shielded ZEC
description: Pay with USDC or SOL from Phantom; receive ZEC in a shielded address.
---

If you have no ZEC on Solana, you can pay with USDC or SOL from the same Phantom wallet. The transaction sends exactly the
quoted USDC (or SOL, as a plain transfer) to a NEAR Intents deposit address; NEAR Intents swaps it and pays native ZEC to
your shielded address.

- You see **the least ZEC you will receive** before you sign. If the price moves more than 1% before the swap completes,
  NEAR Intents refunds your wallet instead.
- The price refreshes every 30 seconds on the buy screen. When you sign, the page takes a new quote and refuses to go on
  if it is more than 1% worse than the one you saw.
- **Small orders.** The bridge's fixed network fee (0.00032 ZEC) is a large share of a small order. The page shows the
  share before you sign when the order is under $10.
- **Minimum order.** Read from 1Click before every quote: about 1.79 USDC or 0.0148 SOL in October 2026 ([sources](/sources#oneclick)).
- **SOL reserve.** When paying with SOL, the page keeps enough SOL in your wallet for fees and Solana's minimum balance.

Fees are on the [fees](/fees) page.
