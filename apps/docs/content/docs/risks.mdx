## ZecDoor is new

Moves and buys are not open yet, and no user moves have been made. They open after our own mainnet test moves pass
([testing](/testing)). ZecDoor has not had an external security audit; its checks are covered by public tests
([security](/security)).

## Bridged ZEC depends on the bridge

ZEC on Solana is a token issued by NEAR's Omni Bridge, backed by ZEC the bridge holds on Zcash. Its value depends on that
bridge working and staying solvent. The bridge's Solana program can be upgraded by a single key ([sources](/sources#bridge)). On 1 October 2026 NEAR Intents
was exploited for about $3.8 million; the funds were returned ([sources](/sources#incidents)).

## Moves can be held, delayed or refused

While a transfer is in progress, the assets may be held, locked or controlled by NEAR Intents' infrastructure. NEAR Intents
screens transfers and may delay, block, freeze or reject them (1Click terms §7.7). One user reported $589,000 held for more
than 50 days, on a move from Zcash to USDT through NEAR Intents ([sources](/sources#incidents)). ZecDoor cannot speed up, release or see into such a hold.

## Moves are irreversible

A completed move cannot be undone. Network fees, the bridge fee, slippage within the quote's tolerance and our fee are not
returned. Quotes expire. NEAR Intents does not consider recovering losses under USD 300 caused by user error.

## Your keys, your responsibility

If you lose the 24 words of a wallet made in ZecDoor, nobody — including us — can recover the ZEC. Write them on paper and
keep them safe. Anyone who has them can spend your ZEC. The same applies to your Phantom wallet.

## Privacy has limits

The move's Solana side, the pool entry, and the link between your Solana wallet and the Zcash address paid are public. See
[what stays public](/what-is-public).

## Software and networks

ZecDoor, Phantom, NEAR Intents, Jupiter, Solana and Zcash can each have bugs, outages or upgrades. The Zcash network is
targeted to upgrade (NU7, mainnet target 5 November 2026, [sources](/sources#zcash)); wallets and servers may need updates around it. NEAR Intents may pause
transfers; ZecDoor then stops offering moves.

provided as is, under the MIT licence.

lawfully where you are.
