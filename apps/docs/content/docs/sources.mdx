with `getProgramAccounts`. Reproduce: [`scripts/stats/solana-zec-holders.mjs`](https://github.com/ZecDoor/zecdoor/blob/main/scripts/stats/solana-zec-holders.mjs);
the output we used is [`docs/stats-2026-10-06.json`](https://github.com/ZecDoor/zecdoor/blob/main/docs/stats-2026-10-06.json).

| Tier | Token accounts | Share | ZEC held | Of which owned by a wallet (not a pool or program) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| At or above the bridge minimum (0.00133669 ZEC): a plain move | 47,236 | 34.2% | 99,536.73 | 36,397 accounts, 88,408.86 ZEC |
| Below the minimum but worth more than a move costs: the top-up | 17,647 | 12.8% | 14.52 | 15,652 accounts, 12.83 ZEC |
| Dust: worth less than a move costs | 73,312 | 53.0% | 7.25 | 64,935 accounts, 6.69 ZEC |
| **All non-empty accounts** | **138,195** | | **99,558.50** | |

**"Worth moving" means worth more than the fixed costs of one move**, which were 47,178 zat (0.00047178 ZEC, about
$0.63) at that time: the bridge's payout fee as quoted (32,000 zat, a ceiling), the deposit address's token account and
the Solana fee (1,503,440 lamports, 13,508 zat at NEAR Intents' prices of $1,340.52 per ZEC and $120.45 per SOL), our
0.25% of the minimum (334 zat) and the top-up swap's 1% slippage limit (1,336 zat). Below that line, moving a balance
costs more than it is worth, and the app says so. The line moves with prices and the bridge's terms; the script reads
them live.

What this means: almost all Solana ZEC (99.98%) is in accounts that can already use the bridge, so ZecDoor's main job for
them is one signature into a shielded wallet, with a proof of arrival. The top-up matters for the 17,647 accounts in the
middle tier. More than half of all accounts hold dust that is not worth moving with any route, ours included.

Token accounts are not people: one person can hold several, exchanges hold many users' balances in one, and pools are
programs. Token supply cross-checked with Solana `getTokenSupply` and
[Jupiter's token API](https://lite-api.jup.ag/tokens/v2/search?query=A7bdiYdS5GjqGFtxf17ppRHtDKPkkRqbKtR27dxvQXaS).
The earlier snapshot ([`docs/stats-2026-10-05.json`](https://github.com/ZecDoor/zecdoor/blob/main/docs/stats-2026-10-05.json))
counted every balance below the minimum together (87,307 of 134,074 accounts); it did not separate dust.

## NEAR Intents 1Click [#oneclick]

- API, quote and status formats: [1Click API docs](https://docs.near-intents.org/integration/distribution-channels/1click-api/about-1click-api.md),
  [OpenAPI spec](https://1click.chaindefuser.com/docs/v0/openapi.yaml).
- **Minimum:** 1Click answers "Amount is too low for bridge, try at least 133334" with no app fee and "…133669" with
  ZecDoor's 25 bps fee (basis points: 100 bps = 1%); the lowest amounts it accepted were 133,011 and 133,345 zat. Hands-on, 5 Oct 2026 (live tests in
  `packages/solana`).
- **Bridge network fee:** `withdrawFee` 32,000 zat (0.00032 ZEC) in every quote we requested, Oct 2026. It is a ceiling:
  on our first mainnet move the payout cost 25,000 zat ([testing](/testing#mainnet-transactions)).
- **Buy minimums:** about 1.79 USDC and 0.0148 SOL. Hands-on, Oct 2026.
- **Orchard-receiver addresses accepted; Sapling-only and `zs1` refused** ("recipient is not valid"). Hands-on, Oct 2026.
- **Quote signature key and construction:** NEAR Intents' SDK `@defuse-protocol/one-click-sdk-typescript` 0.1.26
  (`quoteHash`, `verifyQuoteSignature`). Our implementation is tested against it on real quotes.
- **Status values and refunds:** [status docs](https://docs.near-intents.org/api-reference/oneclick/check-swap-execution-status.md).
- **Terms:** [1Click Terms of Service](https://docs.near-intents.org/security-compliance/terms-of-service.md)
  (§4.1(e) data retention, §4.2 end-user terms, §6.7 quote improvement, §7.7 holds, §9.1 prohibited jurisdictions,
  §12.4 recovery floor).

## Timing [#timing]

- **NEAR Intents' own estimate.** Every 1Click quote carries `timeEstimate`. Solana ZEC → Zcash quotes we read on
  7 October 2026 said 135 seconds (2 minutes 15 seconds); our first mainnet move's quote said the same.
- **Our own runs.** Our first mainnet move arrived 2 minutes 13 seconds after signing ([testing](/testing)).
- **It can take longer.** If a move can't complete, NEAR Intents refunds it to the sending wallet at the quote's deadline;
  our deliberate short deposit was refunded after 31 minutes, at its deadline ([testing](/testing)).
- We give no other typical or usual duration: these three are what we can support.

## Fees [#fees]

- **Fee split.** Live quotes on 5 Oct 2026: on Solana ZEC → ZEC, our requested fee comes back as one row (ours, all of it);
  on USDC and SOL, a 50 bps request comes back as 25 bps for us and 25 bps for NEAR Intents. NEAR's
  [fee documentation](https://docs.near-intents.org/resources/fees.md) describes something different for apps without a
  key; we follow what the service actually does and re-check it every 30 minutes.
- **Solana rent:** `getMinimumBalanceForRentExemption(165)` = 1,488,440 lamports (about 0.0015 SOL, the token account
  each move opens for the deposit address); for a wallet (0 bytes), 650,240. Hands-on, 5 Oct 2026.

## Transaction size and Solana fees [#limits]

- **1,232 bytes** is the size limit for the v0 transactions Phantom signs; Phantom does not yet sign the larger v1
  format ([solana.com](https://solana.com/upgrades/larger-transaction-sizes),
  [phantom-connect-sdk#407](https://github.com/phantom/phantom-connect-sdk/issues/407), Oct 2026).
- **ZecDoor keeps transactions under 1,060 bytes** because Phantom may add Lighthouse assertion instructions after its
  own simulation ([Phantom docs](https://docs.phantom.com/developer-powertools/lighthouse)). Measured on mainnet
  transactions that carry them (`scripts/mainnet/inspect-landed.ts`, 6 Oct 2026): the first check costs about 62
  bytes, because the Lighthouse program's address (`L2TExMFKdjpN9kozasaurPirfHy9P8sbXoAN1qA3S95`) must be in the
  transaction itself, and each further check about 24; a four-check guard took 134 bytes. 1,060 leaves 172. If a top-up's
  Jupiter route would go over, the app asks Jupiter for a route with fewer accounts.
- **Solana network fee:** 5,000 lamports per signature ([Solana docs](https://solana.com/docs/core/fees)) plus the
  priority fee ZecDoor sets (20,000 micro-lamports per compute unit, at most 7,000 lamports for a top-up's 350,000-unit
  limit) — about 0.00001 SOL.

## Bridge [#bridge]

- **The token is NEAR's Omni Bridge ZEC.** Its mint authority is a program-derived address of the Omni Bridge Solana
  program `dahPEoZGXfyV58JqqH85okdHmpN8U2q8owgPUXSCPxe`, listed as Omni Bridge Solana mainnet in the
  [omni-bridge README](https://github.com/Near-One/omni-bridge). The bridge holds real ZEC on Zcash to back it and its
  Solana leg runs on Wormhole messages and NEAR Chain Signatures ([ZecHub guide](https://zechub.wiki/using-zcash/solana-zec-to-shielded)).
- **The program is upgradeable by a single key.** Owner `BPFLoaderUpgradeab1e11111111111111111111111`, program data
  `46ne7xLaLgUwqBTGyezWcgyrvzpXfTPZZkkKR56d5Rhe`, upgrade authority `5kx8AapW8tPkiFbuHiuEBcmN3ddwpBWZfCrZgxXgWeb6`. Read
  from Solana with `getAccountInfo`, hands-on, 5 Oct 2026. Whether that key is a multisig is not known to us.

## Rivals [#rivals]

All read on 5 Oct 2026.

- **Sparkz:** [sparkz.xyz](https://www.sparkz.xyz) ("Shield your Solana ZEC"). Its FAQ: NEAR Intents provides "a one-time
  deposit address on Solana. You send your ZEC there in a single Phantom transaction", and a minimum of "about 0.0013 ZEC".
  Its page script makes a 24-word phrase in the browser with a "Check your backup" step and a birthday height, requires
  an Orchard receiver and refuses unified addresses with a transparent receiver; arrival is followed by NEAR Intents
  status only. **Fee not published:** a quote for 0.1 ZEC gave 0.09928 ZEC out, against 0.09968 for a NEAR Intents
  quote with no app fee, a difference of about 0.4% (our inference). No public source repository found; operator not
  named on the site.
- **CipherSwap:** [cipherswap.app](https://cipherswap.app), source [github.com/Kenbak/cipherswap](https://github.com/Kenbak/cipherswap):
  NEAR Intents 1Click; one Solana transaction signed in Phantom or Solflare (`hooks/useWallet.ts`), or a manual deposit;
  `AFFILIATE_FEE_BPS = 50` (`app/api/swap/quote/route.ts`, unchanged since the first commit), of which its own
  how-it-works page says 0.25% goes to a second account set by 1Click; destination check by prefix and length that also
  accepts `t1`/`t3` and `zs` addresses; status polling only; no licence file (GitHub API `license: null`). Latest commit
  1 Oct 2026 (redesign, no fee change).
- **Zodl:** Solana ZEC as a swap source since iOS 3.11.0 (zodl-ios
  [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/zodl-inc/zodl-ios/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md), 3 Sep 2026: "Swap and Pay now offer … ZEC on
  Solana"); on Android the swap lists 1Click's token list, so no version is tied to it. **67 bps** in Zodl's source
  (`zashiFeeBps = 67` on iOS, `AFFILIATE_FEE_BPS = 67` on Android) and observed on a real Zodl Solana ZEC move: NEAR Intents
  status for deposit address `CETPvNMBKLeyJj3mVnZVxmK7Z1KKr6GPnF8pm6zEWbJS` shows referral `zodl` and an app fee of 67 bps
  (hands-on, 4 Oct 2026). Licence: MIT for zodl-ios and zodl-android (GitHub API).
- **ZecHub's guide** ([zechub.wiki](https://zechub.wiki/using-zcash/solana-zec-to-shielded)) describes the two-site route
  through solswap.org and near.com, with a Sign Message step on near.com; unchanged since 29 Sep 2026.
