## The route

```
Your Phantom wallet ──► one-time NEAR Intents deposit address (Solana)
                         └─► NEAR Intents pays native ZEC ──► your shielded address (Ironwood pool)
```

<Callout title="Opening soon">
Moves and buys open once our own mainnet test moves have passed ([testing](/testing)). Until then the app shows each step
below up to the review, with a live quote, but cannot ask Phantom to sign.
</Callout>

**Ironwood** is Zcash's current shielded pool: it went live in July 2026 and replaced Orchard, which now only accepts
withdrawals. Addresses still carry an "Orchard receiver" to receive into it ([sources](/sources#zcash)).

ZecDoor never holds the funds at any point. If a move cannot complete, NEAR Intents refunds the Solana wallet that sent
it, because every quote ZecDoor asks for names that wallet as the refund address.

## Step by step

<Steps>
<Step>
### You pick the amount and the destination

Choose where the ZEC lands: a new wallet the page makes for you (each move pays a fresh address of it), or a shielded
address you paste from Zodl, Zkool or another Zcash wallet. Pasted addresses are checked in the browser: they must be
unified addresses (starting `u1`) that include an Orchard receiver, because that is what NEAR Intents can pay
([sources](/sources#oneclick)). Older shielded (`zs1…`), public "transparent" (`t1…`, `tex1…`) and test-network
addresses are refused before any quote is asked for.
</Step>
<Step>
### The page checks the bridge and your region

It asks our server whether NEAR Intents reports an incident and whether ZecDoor is offered where you are
([availability](/availability)). It also downloads the US Treasury (OFAC) list of sanctioned Solana addresses and checks your wallet
against it in the browser, so your address is not sent to us.
</Step>
<Step>
### NEAR Intents signs a quote

The browser asks NEAR Intents' 1Click API (its programming interface for swaps) directly for a quote: exactly your amount in, your Zcash address as recipient, your
Solana wallet as refund address, a 30-minute deadline. 1Click answers with a one-time deposit address and an ed25519
signature. The page verifies that signature and every field it covers, and checks the fields it does not cover (the fee
rows and the request deadline) against what it asked for. See [the proof](/proof#1-the-quote-signature).
</Step>
<Step>
### The page builds one transaction and checks it

The transaction may only contain what the [instruction allowlist](/security#the-instruction-allowlist) permits: send the
quoted amount from your own account to the quoted deposit address (and, for a top-up, one Jupiter swap into your own ZEC
account). Then it is simulated against current Solana state; the deposit address must receive exactly the quoted amount.
If any check fails, Phantom is never asked.
</Step>
<Step>
### You sign once in Phantom

Phantom shows one transaction and you approve it once. There is no message to sign and no token approval left behind.
</Step>
<Step>
### The page follows the move

It reads the status from NEAR Intents every few seconds: deposit seen, converting, sent on Zcash. Across 45 mainnet
moves made by other apps through NEAR Intents, which we timed from its status API, this took about 2½ to 9 minutes,
typically about 3 ([sources](/sources#timing)). You can close the page; the move continues, and the page picks up where it
left off when you reopen it on the same device (for a wallet made here, that is also where it can find the note).
</Step>
<Step>
### Your browser finds the note

For a wallet made here, the page downloads recent Zcash blocks from a public server (lightwalletd, at zec.rocks) and tries
your viewing key on each payment in them, all inside your browser. When it finds the payment ("note") at your address
with at least the quoted amount, it shows the [proof](/proof).
</Step>
<Step>
### After arrival

To spend, restore your 24 words and the Wallet Birthday Height in Zodl or Zkool ([wallet](/wallet#spending)). Wait a while
before moving the ZEC again, and don't send out exactly the amount that arrived ([habits](/what-is-public#habits-that-help)).
</Step>
</Steps>

## What runs where

| Part | Runs in | Talks to |
|---|---|---|
| Quote, checks, transaction building, simulation | Your browser | NEAR Intents 1Click, Solana RPC, Jupiter (top-up only) |
| Seed, viewing key, addresses, arrival scan | Your browser (WebAssembly) | A lightwalletd gRPC-web server (zec.rocks) |
| Health, region, serving the sanctions list (checked in your browser), counter | Our server (a Cloudflare Worker) | NEAR Intents' status page and status API |

The complete list of services the app can reach is in its Content Security Policy, quoted in [Security](/security#content-security-policy).
