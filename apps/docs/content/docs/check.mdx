---
title: Check any wallet
description: See what a Solana address holds in bridged ZEC and whether moving it is worth it, without connecting a wallet.
---

Open **[zecdoor.0xo.in/app/#/check](https://zecdoor.0xo.in/app/#/check)** and paste a Solana address. No wallet is connected and nothing can be
signed from that page.

## What it shows

- The ZEC the address holds on Solana, and about what it is worth at NEAR Intents' current price.
- The bridge minimum, read live from NEAR Intents, and whether the balance clears it.
- What ZecDoor would do, with its costs:

| The balance is | ZecDoor would | Shown |
|---|---|---|
| At or above the minimum | Move all of it in one signature | A signed NEAR Intents quote: what arrives shielded at least, the bridge payout fee (an upper figure), our 0.25%, NEAR Intents' own estimate of the time |
| Below the minimum, worth more than one move costs | [Top up](/docs/top-up) with SOL or USDC and move all of it, in the same signature | About how much to swap in, and the fixed cost of one move |
| Worth less than one move costs | Nothing we recommend | **Not worth moving**: what the balance is worth against what a move costs |
| Empty | Nothing to move | A link to [buy shielded ZEC](/docs/buy) |

The fixed cost of one move is the same figure the [holder breakdown](/docs/sources#holders) uses: the bridge's payout fee,
the deposit account's rent and the Solana fee, our fee and the swap's 1% limit.

It reads the address's associated ZEC token account, the standard one wallets receive into. ZEC in any other token account
of the same address is not counted, and ZecDoor would not move it.

## Who sees the address

- **Public Solana RPC endpoints** your browser asks for the balances, as any explorer would. They are not ours.
- **Not our server.** The address is kept in the link after the `#`, which browsers do not send to any server, and the
  page never sends it to us.
- **Not NEAR Intents.** The minimum and the quote are asked for with a throwaway address made in your browser; neither
  depends on whose balance it is. An automated test checks that no request to our server or to NEAR Intents contains the
  address.

Nothing is stored. A shared link names the address, so anyone who opens it sees the same result, read fresh.
