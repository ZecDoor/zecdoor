Every amount below is shown on the review screen before you sign.

| Cost | Move or top-up (Solana ZEC) | Buy with USDC or SOL | Who receives it |
|---|---|---|---|
| ZecDoor fee | 0.25% of the amount | 0.5% of the amount | Moves: all to ZecDoor's NEAR account. Buys: 0.25% to ZecDoor's NEAR account, 0.25% kept by NEAR Intents |
| Bridge network fee | up to 0.00032 ZEC per move | up to 0.00032 ZEC per move | NEAR Intents, for the Zcash withdrawal. The quote's figure is a ceiling: on our first mainnet move it cost 0.00025 ZEC ([the run](/testing#mainnet-transactions)) |
| Swap price and route | — (top-up: Jupiter's route price) | NEAR Intents' quote price | Liquidity providers |
| Solana network fee | about 0.00001 SOL | about 0.00001 SOL | Solana validators |
| Token account for the deposit address | about 0.0015 SOL | about 0.0015 SOL (USDC only) | Paid once per move to open a token account for the deposit address. Solana requires this deposit ("rent"); it stays with that account and does not come back to you |

Figures are from 1Click quotes, Solana's fee rules and Solana's rent on 5 October 2026
([sources](/sources#fees), [Solana fees](/sources#limits)).

**Example, approximate** (the review screen shows exact amounts): a minimum-size move of 0.00133669 ZEC pays about
0.0000033 ZEC to ZecDoor (0.25%) and up to 0.00032 ZEC to the bridge, so at least about 0.00101 ZEC arrives, plus about
0.0015 SOL for the token account and 0.00001 SOL in Solana fees. On a minimum-size move the bridge's fixed fee is about a
quarter of the amount.

**The bridge network fee is an upper figure.** 1Click's quote sets `withdrawFee` to 32,000 zat, and the quote's minimum
amount is worked out with it, so the minimum is what you are promised. The real payout can cost less. On our first
mainnet move (B1, 5 October 2026) the bridge charged 25,000 zat: the shielded note held 0.00108335 ZEC, 7,000 zat more
than the status's `amountOut` of 0.00101335 and above the quote's minimum of 0.00100320
([Zcash transaction `2fac74…f206d6`](https://blockchair.com/zcash/transaction/2fac74310c294c306f56bb9d60891af9d0f8f2ff9c4244f77e1939cf7df206d6),
[the run](/testing#mainnet-transactions)). One move is not a rule: the app always shows the quote's figure as "up to".

## How the ZecDoor fee is charged

Through 1Click's `appFees` field only. **No transfer to ZecDoor ever appears in your transaction** — the allowlist would
refuse it. NEAR Intents takes the fee out of the swap and credits ZecDoor's NEAR account
(`0b419bed99105b07bdcdd9b9f7a6b549fd9d1aab5ac062adbd3725cd6be24a56`).

On Solana ZEC → ZEC, NEAR Intents passes on all of the fee we request. On USDC and SOL, it keeps half: we request 0.5% and
receive 0.25% (observed in live quotes, which differ from NEAR's documentation; [sources](/sources#fees)). Our server
re-checks this split every 30 minutes and the app stops offering a kind of move if it changes.

**Privacy cost of the fee:** because every ZecDoor move pays the same fee account, NEAR Intents' explorer can group ZecDoor
moves together. See [what is public](/what-is-public).

## Quote improvement

NEAR Intents' terms (§6.7) require us to tell you this: if the market moves **against** you within the quote's slippage
tolerance, you bear it; if it moves **in your favour**, NEAR Intents may keep part of the improvement (its "Capture Share",
about half). You always receive at least the minimum shown in the signed quote.

## Refunds

If a move cannot complete, NEAR Intents refunds the Solana wallet that sent it. A refund may carry a fee, shown as the
quote's refund fee. NEAR Intents does not consider recovering losses under USD 300 caused by user error (terms §12.4), such
as sending to a wrong address — which is one reason ZecDoor builds the transaction itself and checks the address first.
