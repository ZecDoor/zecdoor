---
title: A first Zcash wallet
description: Made in your browser, backed up on paper, opened later in Zodl or Zkool.
---

If you have no Zcash wallet yet, ZecDoor can make one in the page.

## What happens

1. The page asks a public Zcash server (lightwalletd) for the current block height and makes 24 recovery words (the
   standard BIP-39 format) inside your browser.
2. You reveal the words when nobody is watching and write them on paper. **There is no copy button, on purpose**:
   clipboards and screenshots can be read by other apps.
3. You also write down the **Wallet Birthday Height**: the block the wallet was made at. Enter it whenever you restore the
   wallet, in Zodl or Zkool, so it starts looking for your ZEC from that block.
4. The page asks you to pick three of the words from your paper. You cannot continue until all three are right.
5. The page derives a **viewing key** (Orchard-type, which also sees the Ironwood pool) and keeps only that, in this
   browser's storage (IndexedDB). The 24 words leave
   memory as soon as the check passes. They are never written to storage and never sent anywhere.

## Fresh addresses

Each move into this wallet pays a new address of it (the next one in the wallet's sequence). All of them belong to the
same wallet: your 24 words open every one of them. Someone who knows one of your Zcash addresses cannot use it to find your
other payments. All of them are still linked, on NEAR Intents' explorer, to the Solana wallet that paid them
([what is public](/what-is-public)).

## Spending

ZecDoor never spends. To spend, open the wallet in a full Zcash wallet app:

1. Install **Zodl** ([zodl.com](https://zodl.com)) or **Zkool** ([hhanh00.github.io/zkool2](https://hhanh00.github.io/zkool2/),
   source at [github.com/hhanh00/zkool2](https://github.com/hhanh00/zkool2)), from those links, in a version that supports
   Ironwood.
2. Choose to restore a wallet, enter your 24 words, and enter the Wallet Birthday Height the page showed you.
3. Let it sync. Your ZEC appears in the shielded balance.
4. **Check the restore.** On ZecDoor's "What to do next" screen, paste a receiving address from the restored wallet (in
   Zodl: Receive). Your browser checks, with the viewing key it kept, that the address belongs to the same wallet, and
   tells you its address number. A wallet app shows addresses with Sapling and transparent parts next to the Orchard
   one; only the Orchard part is checked, and it is enough. ZecDoor never asks for your words to do this.

We tested the check against a real restore: the address zcash-devtool (built on the same Rust wallet libraries as Zodl's
wallets) derived from our first mainnet move's 24 words is recognised as address number 0 of the wallet the browser made.

## Forgetting the wallet on this device

The "Forget this wallet" button removes the viewing key from this browser. Your ZEC stays in the shielded pool; your 24
words still open it. Do it only once your words are safe. If you forget it before a move arrives, the page can no longer
show the proof; check the arrival in Zodl or Zkool instead.

## What the viewing key can and cannot do

It can see every payment into and out of this wallet, past and future (that is how the page proves arrival), and so could
anyone who gets this browser's storage. It cannot spend. It stays on your device, and "Forget this wallet" removes it.
