---
title: A first Zcash wallet
description: Made in your browser, backed up on paper, opened later in Zodl or Zkool.
---

If you have no Zcash wallet yet, ZecDoor can make one in the page.

## What happens

1. The page asks a lightwalletd server for the current block height and generates 24 words (BIP-39) in WebAssembly.
2. You reveal the words when nobody is watching and write them on paper. **There is no copy button, on purpose**:
   clipboards and screenshots can be read by other apps.
3. You also write down the **Wallet Birthday Height** (a block number). Zodl asks for it on restore; in Zkool, type it
   in — left blank, Zkool starts from today and misses earlier ZEC.
4. The page asks you to pick three of the words from your paper. You cannot continue until all three are right.
5. The page derives an **Orchard-only viewing key** and keeps only that, in this browser's IndexedDB. The 24 words leave
   memory as soon as the check passes. They are never written to storage and never sent anywhere.

## Fresh addresses

Each move into this wallet pays a new address of it (the next diversifier index). All of them belong to the same wallet:
your 24 words open every one of them. Because NEAR Intents' explorer shows which Zcash address a Solana wallet paid, new
addresses keep those payments from collecting on one address ([what is public](/what-is-public)).

## Spending

ZecDoor never spends. To spend, restore your 24 words and the birthday height in a full wallet:

- **Zodl** — [zodl.com](https://zodl.com)
- **Zkool** — [hhanh00.github.io/zkool2](https://hhanh00.github.io/zkool2/), source at [github.com/hhanh00/zkool2](https://github.com/hhanh00/zkool2)

## Forgetting the wallet on this device

The "Forget this wallet" button removes the viewing key from this browser. Your ZEC stays in the shielded pool; your 24
words still open it. Do it only once your words are safe.

## What the viewing key can and cannot do

It can see payments to the wallet (that is how the page proves arrival). It cannot spend. It stays on your device.
