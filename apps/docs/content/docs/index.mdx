ZecDoor is open-source, non-custodial software. You open it in Phantom, choose where your ZEC should land, check the
review, and sign one Solana transaction. NEAR Intents' 1Click service then pays native ZEC into the shielded pool, to an
address only you control.

## Why it exists

ZEC on Solana is a bridged SPL token (mint `A7bdiYdS5GjqGFtxf17ppRHtDKPkkRqbKtR27dxvQXaS`). Like every SPL token, its
balances and transfers are public. On 5 October 2026, 134,074 Solana token accounts held it, and 87,307 of them (65.1%)
held less than the 0.00133669 ZEC the bridge will move ([sources](/sources#holders)). ZecDoor exists to get that ZEC into
the shielded pool, including the small balances.

## What ZecDoor does that others don't

- **Top-up for small balances.** A Jupiter swap fills the gap to the bridge minimum inside the same transaction.
- **A first wallet, backed up properly.** 24 words made in your browser, a three-word check, and the birthday height
  Zodl and Zkool need.
- **Proof you can check.** NEAR Intents' signed quote, both transaction IDs, and the shielded note found by your own
  browser with your viewing key.
- **Opens inside Phantom** from a link on your phone.
- **MIT-licensed code** and a **public counter** that stores no addresses.

CipherSwap and Zodl's built-in swap already move Solana ZEC to shielded Zcash in one step. [How they compare](/comparison).

## What ZecDoor never does

- Hold your funds. Every move goes from your wallet to a NEAR Intents deposit address to your Zcash address.
- See your keys or your 24 words. They never leave the page.
- Ask you to sign a message, or approve a token allowance.
- Store your Solana or Zcash address on our server.
- Run analytics or tracking scripts.
- Sell a token. There is none.
