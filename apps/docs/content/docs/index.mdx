ZecDoor is open-source, non-custodial software. You open it in Phantom, choose where your ZEC should land, check the
review, and sign one Solana transaction. NEAR Intents (a cross-chain swap service; 1Click is its programming interface)
then pays real ZEC on the Zcash network into the shielded pool, Zcash's encrypted part, to an address only you control.

<Callout title="Opening soon">
Moves and buys are not open yet. Today you can connect Phantom, see your balance and live quotes, and make and back up a
wallet. Signing opens once our own mainnet test moves have passed ([testing](/testing)).
</Callout>

**To start:** on your phone, open [zecdoor.0xo.in](https://zecdoor.0xo.in) and tap "Open in Phantom", or open
`zecdoor.0xo.in/app` in the browser inside Phantom. On a computer, the Phantom extension works the same.

## Why it exists

ZEC on Solana is a bridged token (SPL is Solana's token standard; its "mint", the token's ID, is
`A7bdiYdS5GjqGFtxf17ppRHtDKPkkRqbKtR27dxvQXaS`). Like every SPL token, its
balances and transfers are public. On 6 October 2026, 138,195 Solana token accounts held it. 47,236 of them, holding
99.98% of it, are at or above the bridge minimum; 17,647 are below the minimum but worth more than a move costs (about
$0.63); 73,312 hold dust worth less than that ([sources](/sources#holders)). ZecDoor exists to get Solana ZEC into the
shielded pool in one signature, with the top-up for the middle group, and to say plainly when a balance is not worth
moving.

## What ZecDoor adds

- **Top-up for small balances.** A Jupiter swap fills the gap to the bridge minimum inside the same transaction.
- **A first wallet, backed up properly.** 24 words made in your browser, a three-word check, and the Wallet Birthday
  Height (the block the wallet was made at, so a restore knows where to start) that Zodl and Zkool, two Zcash wallet
  apps, need.
- **Proof you can check.** NEAR Intents' signed quote, both transaction IDs, and the shielded note found by your own
  browser with your viewing key (a key that can see, but not spend, your ZEC).
- **Opens inside Phantom** from a link on your phone.
- **MIT-licensed code** and a **public counter** that stores no addresses.

Sparkz, CipherSwap and Zodl's built-in swap already move Solana ZEC to shielded Zcash in one step, and Sparkz also
makes a first wallet in the page. [How they compare](/comparison).

## What ZecDoor never does

- Hold your funds. Every move goes from your wallet to a NEAR Intents deposit address to your Zcash address.
- See your keys or your 24 words. They never leave the page.
- Ask you to sign a message, or approve a token allowance.
- Store your Solana or Zcash address on our server.
- Run analytics or tracking scripts.
- Sell a token. There is none.
