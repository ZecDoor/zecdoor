title: Top up and shield
description: How a balance below the bridge minimum moves anyway, in the same single transaction.
---

NEAR Intents only takes Solana ZEC deposits above a minimum. With ZecDoor's 0.25% fee, 1Click suggests at least
**0.00133669 ZEC** (it reads "try at least 133669"; the lowest amount it actually accepted on 5 October 2026 was 133,345 zat —
ZecDoor uses the suggested figure and re-reads it before every move). Two in three Solana ZEC accounts hold less than that
([sources](/sources#holders)).

## What the one transaction does

1. **Swap.** Jupiter (Metis) swaps just enough SOL or USDC into ZEC, as an *exact-output* swap: the output is exactly the
   missing amount, and the most you can pay is fixed by the quote's 1% slippage bound.
2. **Send.** All of your ZEC — what you had plus what the swap bought — goes to the NEAR Intents deposit address for your
   shielded address.

If any part fails, the whole transaction fails and nothing moves. Phantom shows both parts; that is expected.

## A real example

A read-only simulation against mainnet on 5 October 2026, for a wallet holding 37,814 zat (0.00037814 ZEC):

| | |
|---|---|
| Missing to the minimum | 95,855 zat |
| Jupiter route | SOL → ZEC, exact output |
| SOL spent | 0.010558068 SOL |
| Sent to the deposit address | 133,669 zat |
| Transaction size | 1,030 bytes (limit 1,232; ZecDoor stays under 1,100) |
| Compute used | 147,488 units |

Simulations use `sigVerify: false` and send nothing. They are part of the test suite ([testing](/testing)).

## What the allowlist allows in a top-up

On top of the normal deposit, and nothing else:

- one Jupiter `exact_out_route` or `shared_accounts_exact_out_route` (v1 or v2) whose **destination is your own ZEC
  account**, whose authority is you, whose input is your own SOL or USDC account, and which carries **no platform fee
  account**. The account layout is checked against Jupiter's on-chain IDL.
- wrapping your own SOL into your own wrapped-SOL account, capped at the swap's maximum input, and closing it again in the
  same transaction.
- creating your own ZEC account if you do not have one yet (you pay its rent; it stays yours).

The complete rules are in [Security](/security#the-instruction-allowlist).

## Where it is not offered

Jupiter's terms restrict the United States, China, Singapore, Côte d'Ivoire and Iraq. ZecDoor does not offer the top-up there.
Moves of balances already above the minimum still work. Whether Jupiter's interface restriction binds API integrators is
UNVERIFIED; ZecDoor applies it anyway ([availability](/availability)).
