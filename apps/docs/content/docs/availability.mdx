ZecDoor checks your region before every move, from the country our host (Cloudflare) assigns to your connection. Nothing
about the request is stored.

## Not offered

Under NEAR Intents' terms (1Click Terms of Service §9.1), ZecDoor is not offered in: Afghanistan, Belarus, the Central
African Republic, Cuba, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Guinea-Bissau, Haiti, Iran, Libya, Mali, Myanmar, Nicaragua,
North Korea, Russia, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Venezuela, Yemen, Zimbabwe, and the Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk,
Zaporizhzhia and Kherson regions of Ukraine and Sevastopol. It is not offered to anyone on US, EU, UK or UN sanctions lists,
and wallets on OFAC's list of sanctioned Solana addresses are refused.

Using a VPN or other means to get around these restrictions is not allowed (see the [Terms](/legal/terms)).

Whether Cloudflare reliably identifies the Ukrainian regions is UNVERIFIED.

## Top-up not offered

The top-up uses Jupiter, whose terms restrict the United States, China, Singapore, Côte d'Ivoire and Iraq. ZecDoor hides
the top-up there. Moves of balances above the bridge minimum, and buys, remain available where NEAR Intents allows them.

## United Kingdom

ZecDoor is offered in the UK. You use it on your own initiative; neither ZecDoor nor NEAR Intents recommends trading or
holding crypto-assets, and nothing on this site is an inducement to do so. ZecDoor is not authorised by the Financial
Conduct Authority. Crypto-assets are high-risk and you could lose all the money you put in.

## European Union

ZecDoor is non-custodial software and does not hold or control your assets. See the [risks](/risks).
