ZecDoor

Move the ZEC you hold on Solana into your own shielded Zcash wallet.

ZecDoor is open-source, non-custodial software. It never holds your funds and never sees your keys. It is being built now and is not ready to use.

Connect test

This page can only connect to Phantom and show your address. It cannot ask you to sign anything — there is no signing code on this site yet.

  • Non-custodial: transfers go from your wallet, through NEAR Intents 1Click, to an address you control.
  • No token. No sign-message step.
  • Source code: github.com/ZecDoor/zecdoor
